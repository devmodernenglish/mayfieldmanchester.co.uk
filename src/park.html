Mayfield Park is a place for all, whether you’re here to relax, play, or explore

A public 6.5-acre park, with 142 trees, 120,000 shrubs and plants. The park features a kids’ play yard with 6 slides woven around the newly daylighted River Medlock – one of Manchester’s founding rivers. Made possible by the Mayfield Partnership.
