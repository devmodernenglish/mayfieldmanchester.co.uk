Spaces to eat, drink, dance, listen and experience Mayfield

A hub of culture and entertainment

Experiences for all, just a stone’s throw away from Mayfield Park

Mayfield offers an ever-evolving mix of industrial history and modern entertainment.

Shifting from high-octane nocturnal club nights, to local culture and iconic heritage pubs and the expansive street-food offering of Freight Island, to immersive family pop-ups and upcoming social sports spaces and vinyl bars.

Mayfield Park is Manchester’s first new public park in more than 100 years. Opening in September 2022, it covers 7.5 acres and is one of the most biodiverse spaces in the city.

Learn more

Freight Island is an oasis of incredible food, drink, music, live sport and immersive entertainment, hidden in plain sight at Manchester’s Depot Mayfield, behind Piccadilly train station.

Visit website

Depot Mayfield is a 10,000 capacity venue in Manchester’s historic former railway station. The site was transformed in 2019 into a multi-use space for arts, music and events of all types.

Visit website

The Star and Garter is not just a Grade II listed pub, but the last truly independent music venue, built in 1803 and relocated to its current location on Fairfield Street in 1877.

Visit website

The 2 decade long Warehouse Project is one of the most iconic club nights in the world, with its main season of events running from September through to New Year’s Day, hosted at Depot.
